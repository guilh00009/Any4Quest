Implementation: reconstruct tangent coordinates from the observed full-view UV,
sample the near and wide portions of the same atlas, and blend linearly with
clamp((length(tangent)-transition_start)/(transition_end-transition_start),0,1).
GDC 2017 AAA on a VR Budget describes a near circle and enlarged blend region:
https://media.gdcvault.com/gdc2017/Presentations/Erjavec_Rok_AAAOnVRBudget.pdf#page=47
The linear radial curve is an explicit approximation, not a verified Sony curve.
